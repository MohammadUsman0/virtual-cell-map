# Contributing

`data/organisations.json` is the source of truth. `docs/` is generated by `scripts/build.py`;
never edit it by hand.

## What belongs here

An organisation is included if it builds, funds or supplies data for AI models that represent or predict
cell states and their responses to perturbations. Closely related organisations may be included with
`category: "Adjacent"` and a `context_note` explaining why. Appearing on a challenge leaderboard is not
enough on its own: such organisations stay in `data/vcc2026_candidates.json` until their own work is found.

## Record format

```json
{
  "id": "arc-institute",
  "name": "Arc Institute",
  "type": "Non-profit research institute",
  "category": "Perturbation models (single-cell)",
  "city": "Palo Alto", "country": "USA",
  "metro": "SF Bay Area", "lat": 37.44, "lon": -122.14,
  "website": "arcinstitute.org",
  "founded": "", "founded_source": null,
  "approach": "Single-cell perturbation",
  "data": "...", "models": "State, Stack, ...",
  "summary": "One or two sentences in your own words.",
  "open_release": "Yes",
  "openness_detail": "...",
  "pharma_partners": [],
  "virtual_cell_challenge": "Host",
  "sources": [
    {"url": "https://...", "supports": "summary, models", "quality": "primary"}
  ],
  "context_note": "",
  "needs_verification": "",
  "last_checked": "2026-11-06"
}
```

- `category`: one of the categories listed in `site/template.html` (`CATS`).
- `open_release`: `Yes`, `Partial`, `No` or `—` (not applicable / unknown).
- `sources`: at least one. `quality` is `primary` (the organisation's own release, paper, repository or
  institute page) or `press`. `supports` says which fields the link backs up.
- `founded`: only together with `founded_source`; leave both empty otherwise.
- `virtual_cell_challenge`: host or sponsor role, 2025 prizes, or a 2026 final rank in the top 10. Nothing else.
- `needs_verification`: anything not confirmed; shown with ⚠ on the page. Never leave doubts silent.
- `metro`, `lat`, `lon`: the metro area the organisation is grouped under on the map.

Funding is not recorded.

## Rules

1. Never invent details. Leave a field empty rather than guess.
2. Write `summary` in your own words.
3. Prefer primary sources; trade press is fine as a second source.
4. Update `last_checked` when you re-check an entry.
5. Run `python scripts/build.py` before opening a pull request. It rejects unknown fields, missing
   sources, unsourced founding years and malformed URLs, and CI fails if `docs/` is out of date.
